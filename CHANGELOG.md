## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Water surface querying, swimming, and drowning (`gtasarust-world`, `gtasarust-entities`, `gtasarust-renderer`, `gtasarust-ui`, `assets/shaders/world.wgsl`): exact 2D triangle barycentric `WorldManager::water_height_at` + ocean outer bounds, CJ `breath` reserve (`100.0`) with buoyancy/swimming damping, drowning damage, hospital breath reset, underwater blue HUD breath gauge, and animated scrolling `waterclear256` shader (`light_params.x > 2.5`).
- Dynamic vehicle kinematic `rapier3d` colliders & fire/explosion lifecycle (`gtasarust-physics`, `gtasarust-vehicles`, `gtasarust-audio`, `gtasarust-game`): per-vehicle kinematic box colliders (`VEHICLE_COLLIDER_TAG_FLAG`) w/ self-tag excluded bumper/wheel/camera raycasts (`raycast_excluding`), boat water buoyancy & deep-water engine stall, `< 250 HP` fire burn timer -> single explosion (`SfxCue::Explosion` mapped to `GENRL` 45/0 + synthesized fallback + charred wreck + blast flash & area damage).
- Over-the-shoulder aiming, handheld weapon attachments, and occluded 3D combat (`gtasarust-renderer`, `gtasarust-animation`, `gtasarust-ui`, `gtasarust-game`): `CameraState::update_over_shoulder` + `aim_ray`, narrowed aiming FOV, `siteM16` aiming reticle, `weapon_hud_sprite_name` active HUD weapon icons, preloaded 9 handheld weapon DFFs (`colt45`..`knifecur`) mounted to `BONE_R_HAND = 24` (`compute_bone_world_matrix`), and wall/vehicle/ped-occluded shooting raycasts.
- Projected ground shadows, `CHudColours` GXT rich text, and SCM screen fade (`gtasarust-localization`, `gtasarust-renderer`, `gtasarust-ui`, `gtasarust-game`): `convert_shadow_luminance_to_alpha` soft alpha conversion for `particle.txd` `shad_*`/`bloodpool_64` textures, `CpuMeshData::unit_shadow_quad` projected onto `.col` terrain (`shad_ped`/`shad_car`), `parse_sa_rich_text` (`~r~/~g~/~b~/~y~/~p~/~w~/~s~/~l~/~h~/~n~`) rendering via `LayoutJob`, and `DoFade`/`DisplayHud`/`DisplayRadar` SCM curtain & UI overrides.
- True GTA:SA visual/lighting pipeline (`assets/shaders/{world,sky}.wgsl`, `gtasarust-renderer`, `gtasarust-world`, `gtasarust-gta-formats`): `ExtraVertColour` (`0x0253F2F9`) night prelit vertex colors blended by `timecyc.dat` `dn_balance`, `ambient_obj_color`, and `water_color`, full 2x2 mipmap pyramids on linear `Rgba8Unorm` textures with sRGB gamma encoding, 3-pass rendering (`world_pipeline`, `world_double_sided_pipeline`, `world_blend_pipeline`), `txdp` parent TXD chains across IDEs and `txd.ide`, `IDE_FLAG_*` cutout/glass/backface routing, hour-based `is_active_at_hour` placements, horizon `is_lod` skyline switching, `bounding_radius` frustum culling, transparent water pass, and removal of synthetic ground quad when real map assets are active.
- Modular skinned CJ from `models/player.img` (`head`, `vest`, `jeans`, `sneaker`, `hands` animated with `ped.ifp`), decoupled third-person mouse orbit camera w/ pointer lock (`Locked`/`Confined`), mouse wheel zoom, `.col` anti-clip raycast (`update_third_person_collided`), camera-relative locomotion/aim yaw, and separated vehicle body + 4 articulated `wheel_*_dummy` mounts w/ steering, rolling spin, left/right flip, and 4-point `.col` pitch/roll ground alignment (`gtasarust-entities`, `gtasarust-vehicles`, `gtasarust-renderer`, `gtasarust-game`).
- Authentic retail UI media (`gtasarust-ui`, `gtasarust-game`): `LOADSCS.TXD` (`loadsc0`) main/pause backdrop w/ `"RESUME FREE ROAM"`, `models/hud.txd` (`69` sprites incl. `fist`, `radardisc`, `radar_north`, `radar_centre`, `radar_sweet`, `radar_cj`), rotating circular radar minimap clipped from `radar00..radar143.txd` tiles (`gta3.img`), and 12x12 fullscreen textured map view.
- Full `ScmImage` chunk parser (`'s'`, `0..=4`) + 699 SA opcode metadata shapes (`scm_opcodes.rs`) derived from `D:\gta-reversed` & retail `main.scm` + multi-thread `ScmVm` bytecode execution (`gtasarust-gta-formats`, `gtasarust-scripting`), loading retail `main.scm` (`main_offset=55976`, `135` missions) and dispatching `ScmWorldCommand` updates into `GameWorldState`.

- `AGENTS.md` and `CONTINUE.md` autonomous resumption and verification handoff guides.

- Dynamic `.col` collision streaming (`10,160` models indexed from `models/coll/*.col`, `gta3.img`, `gta_int.img`), transformed `rapier3d` trimesh/box/sphere static colliders (`25,041` active around Grove Street), on-foot wall sliding, vehicle wall impact damage, and ground-raycast spawning on real Grove Street asphalt (`Z=12.712m`, `BUG-006`).

- Textured RenderWare DFF/TXD per-submesh rendering in `gtasarust-renderer` & `gtasarust-game` (`DffSubMesh`, hierarchical `DffFrame` transforms, vehicle primary/secondary paint tinting, `water.dat` 301-quad ocean/river mesh, and dynamic nearby DFF/TXD sector streaming; verified `68` models / `629` textures preloaded against `E:\gtasa`).
